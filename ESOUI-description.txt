[B]About OneMap[/B]
Map enhancements and social-first travel for ESO's PC keyboard world map.

[LIST]
[*]Optional 10% larger map, hidden Area Completion panel and soft blue waves around your player marker.
[*]Single-line house/location entries and separate Dungeons and Trials tabs.
[*]Left-click quests to see their zone instead of a transport NPC.
[*]Right-click destinations to travel, preferring free jumps through your group, friends and guilds.
[*]Direct dungeon/trial entry, house interiors and the game's priority wayshrines for zones.
[*]Nearest unlocked wayshrines for located antiquities and known quest objectives when no free jump is available.
[*]Travel price or Free in tooltips. Antiquities retain their context menu with an added Teleport action.
[/LIST]

Settings: Settings > Addons > OneMap. English and Russian interface. The game's travel restrictions and confirmation dialogs apply.
